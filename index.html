CROP CODERS • KAGGRICULTURE

Autonomous Farming Intelligence

Crop Coders — Kaggriculture is a modular intelligent farming-agent project designed around observation, prediction, constraints, strategy, action and feedback. The architecture focuses on building a system capable of making decisions inside a turn-based farming environment, evaluating outcomes and improving its strategy.
